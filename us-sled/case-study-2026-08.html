Decoded RFP 2026-08 Decode published Feb 12, 2026

Professional consulting services for Bay County Materials Management Plan.

A pre-bid strategic analysis of a Michigan PA 451 Part 115 Materials Management Plan RFP, with the evaluation math, statutory traps, timeline reality, and team composition primes need to bid on win-theme, not on price.

How we decoded it

Four insights the SOW narrative buries past page 38.

Insight 01 · Evaluation math

Experience weight is 25%. Fee weight is 10%.

Across six Michigan MMP awards since 2022, the evaluation matrix has averaged a 2.5x weight on subject-matter experience versus fee. A fee-priced bid optimizing the bottom of the cost column will systematically lose to a slightly more expensive bid that anchors its win theme on prior MMP work.

Experience 25%
Approach & method 22%
Project team 20%
Engagement plan 15%
References 8%
Fee 10%
ImplicationAnchor the cover letter on three named MMP engagements before mentioning price.
Insight 02 · Compliance trap

The SOW cites Part 115 by reference. The matrix scores by adoption sequence.

Section 3.4 demands "compliance with applicable state statute" without enumerating sub-clauses. The scoring matrix in Appendix C, however, awards points for explicit alignment to the 14-step adoption sequence in EGLE's 2024 MMP Implementation Guidance, not the statute itself. Bids citing PA 451 without mapping to the 14-step sequence consistently score 4 of 14 max on this line.

ImplicationWrite Section 3.4 of your response against the EGLE guidance, not against the statute text.
Insight 03 · Timeline reality

The 24-month performance period assumes a 4-month adoption buffer that doesn't exist.

The RFP specifies plan delivery at month 20 and county adoption by month 24. Michigan PA 451 requires a 90-day public comment period and a 60-day EGLE review window before adoption can be scheduled. The realistic finish-to-adoption gap is 5.5 months, which compresses build-phase delivery from 16 months to 14.5.

ImplicationBid the build against 14.5 months. The 16-month assumption produces deficiency-trigger risk on month 21.
Insight 04 · Team composition

The RFP requests four named resumes. Five is the floor that scores.

Section 4.2 specifies four "key personnel" resumes. The scoring matrix, however, evaluates "team depth including bench redundancy" as a separate sub-criterion. Three of the four 2024-2025 award winners submitted five to seven resumes. A four-resume submission scores at the lower bound of the depth criterion.

ImplicationSubmit five resumes: PM, MMP technical lead, GIS lead, engagement lead, and one bench-redundancy hire.
